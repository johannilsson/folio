# Natural Wine: Producer Notes

## Why Producers Matter

Natural wine is less standardised than conventional wine, so the person making it matters more than the appellation. Two bottles from the same village can taste nothing alike.

## Regions to Know

### Jura

Jura is the home of oxidative whites made from Savagnin, aged under a veil of yeast called flor. The wines are nutty, saline, and unlike anything else in France.

### Loire

The Loire valley is the centre of the modern natural wine movement. Chenin Blanc in particular shows what low-intervention farming can do, from bone-dry to lusciously sweet.

### Georgia

Georgia has made wine in qvevri, buried clay vessels lined with beeswax, for around 6,000 years. White grapes are fermented with their skins, which is where orange wine comes from.

## How to Buy

Find an importer you trust and follow their list. A good importer has already done the tasting, and their name on the back label is the closest thing natural wine has to a quality guarantee.
