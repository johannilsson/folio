# Natural Wine: Tasting Notes

## How to Taste

Look, smell, taste, then think. Give the glass a really good swirl, because many natural wines open up in the first minute. Write down what you notice before you read the label.

## Cellar Log

- Keep bottles on their side in the dark, around 12 °C.
- Open pét-nat bottles over a sink.
- Drink light reds within a year or two of release.
